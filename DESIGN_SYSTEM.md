# Design system

## Direction

Direct, practical, approachable service communication. The visual system uses a navy foundation, blue supporting tones, a gold action accent, white surfaces, and a pale soft section background.

## Tokens

Defined in `assets/site.css`: `--navy`, `--navy-deep`, `--blue`, `--gold`, `--paper`, `--soft`, `--ink`, `--muted`, and `--line`.

## Layout

The site is mobile-first, uses a centered max-width container, responsive grids, intrinsic image dimensions, sticky navigation, and a mobile contact bar. Focus states are visible and reduced-motion preferences are respected.

## Content rules

Keep service claims specific and qualified. Illustrative images must retain their existing disclosure. Preserve the existing direct-contact CTA pattern unless the business owner approves a change.
